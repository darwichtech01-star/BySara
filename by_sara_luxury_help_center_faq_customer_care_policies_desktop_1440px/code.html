QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A little more clarity.

Honest protocols guiding your inquiries, orders, and consultations with BY SARA.

The Inquiry Bag is an editorial selection tray designed to prepare a structured summary of your desired formulations. When you finish adding items to your bag, clicking “Proceed to WhatsApp Review” transfers your itemized selection directly into a confidential dialogue with our Beirut Concierge.

Crucially, opening WhatsApp does NOT charge or execute a financial purchase. It opens a direct, unpressured consultation where Sara and our licensed beauty advisors manually verify fresh vault batch quantities, expiration dates, skin compatibility, and convenient delivery times before you confirm.

Because our maison curates scarce French, Swiss, and Levantine botanical skincare batches, our physical vault inventory changes frequently throughout the day. When you submit your Inquiry Bag via WhatsApp, our team directly inspects the Downtown Beirut physical vault to confirm batch allocation in real time.

If an elixir or treatment cream is temporarily exhausted, we will present immediate alternatives with identical therapeutic actives or reserve an allocation from the next verified shipment.

check_circle No financial commitment: Opening WhatsApp simply drafts a structured message into your chat app. You retain 100% control to review, edit, or cancel.

No payment information is collected on our web platform. You are free to ask questions about texture, compatibility with other products, or adjust quantities. Transactions are concluded exclusively after explicit agreement, with Cash on Delivery or confirmed transfer at handoff.

Every product page features a dedicated Clinical Formulation & INCI Matrix documenting primary actives, biological botanical extracts, and pH parameters. We guarantee complete transparency regarding preservative profiles, cold-pressed extraction methods, and allergy disclosures.

Should you require specific medical patch-test documentation or compatibility insights with retinoids or AHA/BHA exfoliants, our resident skin therapists are available on the care desk chat.

Workflow Architecture

Four simple steps to your vanity

1
Curate Your Desired Formulations

Browse our Face Care, Sun Care, and Fragrance galleries. When a formulation fits your ritual, select “Add to Inquiry Bag”. Your selection persists across your session, allowing you to curate multiple elixirs without urgency.

2
Review Quantities in Your Vanity Tray

Open your Inquiry Bag from the top navigation. You can adjust bottle volumes, remove items, or include special requests (such as personalized gold calligraphy gift wrapping or fragile climate packing).

3
Generate Your WhatsApp Consultation Note

Tapping “Proceed to WhatsApp Review” formats your list into an organized, encrypted dispatch note. It automatically opens WhatsApp on your phone or desktop. You have complete control to examine the message before tapping send.

4
Personal Confirmation & White-Glove Dispatch

Sara’s team immediately answers to confirm current vault inventory, recommend matching complimentary samples, and set your delivery window. No credit card is stored; settlement occurs peacefully in cash or approved transfer upon physical handoff.

01. General Dispatch Overview

BY SARA operates a specialized luxury distribution network designed to protect active cosmetic compounds from thermal degradation, vibration, and prolonged warehousing. All dispatches originate directly from our climate-controlled Downtown Beirut Sanctuary.

02. Delivery Coverage & Beirut Zones

We distinguish our delivery services across three primary service tiers:

Greater Beirut

Same-day hand delivery on inquiries confirmed before 14:00. Complimentary for orders exceeding $100.

Mount Lebanon

24 to 48 hour climate-shielded transit across Metn, Keserwan, and Chouf regions.

Regional Lebanon

North, South, and Bekaa destinations scheduled via private courier on designated weekly runs.

03. Temperature-Preserved Cold-Chain

During summer months (May through October), sensitive formulations containing unpreserved botanical oils, probiotic cultures, or unstable antioxidants are dispatched in thermal insulated pouches with reusable food-grade chill packs.

04. Inspection Upon Handoff

Patrons are invited and encouraged to inspect the tamper-evident wax seal and outer packaging upon delivery before completing cash handoff to our courier. If a bottle shows sign of transit damage or leakage, you may decline acceptance without any penalty or obligation.

05. Care Desk Escalations

Should an inquiry experience unexpected delays due to transit weather or road logistics, our concierge contacts you proactively via WhatsApp with up-to-the-minute updates and revised arrival windows.

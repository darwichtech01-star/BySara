## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-touch, editorial luxury beauty boutique aesthetic rooted in Mediterranean and Middle Eastern sophistication. It balances sensory richness with modern digital clarity, capturing the indulgent warmth of Lebanese hospitality and elite skincare rituals.

### Visual Style
- **Movement:** High-editorial luxury meets tactile minimalism. It combines deep, plush drapery tones (burgundy and deep wine) with airy alabaster canvases (warm ivory, soft blush), framed by hairline gold metallic filigree.
- **Brand Personality:** Regal, discreet, bespoke, sensual, and impeccably curated.
- **Emotional Response:** Indulgence, timeless exclusivity, clinical confidence, and serene pampering.
- **Design Metaphor:** Silk drapery gently unfurling over warm travertine marble, accented with fine gold leaf stampings and luminous dewy textures.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from vintage Lebanese couture velvets, delicate champagne bubbles, and botanical formulations.

### Palette Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary Burgundy (`#3B1024`):** The signature brand statement. Applied to primary CTAs, main editorial headings, luxury badges, and key navigation anchors.
- **Deep Wine (`#270D1A`):** Ultra-rich contrast accent used for footer backdrops, dramatic hero banner overlays, and high-emphasis hover states.
- **Muted Burgundy (`#60293F`):** Intermediate tone for secondary category chips, subtle borders on dark themes, and active tab indicators.
- **Champagne Gold (`#D8B18F`):** Delicate metallic warmth used for iconography, luxury borders, star ratings, promotional frames, and secondary action highlights.
- **Soft Rose Gold (`#C99184`):** Warm terracotta-rose used for tags, product badges (e.g., "Exclusive", "New Formula"), and subtle interactive focus states.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F4`):** The primary canvas surface, providing an organic, warm alternative to sterile paper whites.
- **Cream (`#F5EBDF`):** Secondary container surface for cards, inset panels, and table rows.
- **Soft Blush (`#F4E4E4`):** Delicate tint for hover fills, swatch backdrops, and active promotional pill buttons.
- **Dark Text (`#261923`):** High-legibility deep plum-black replacing pure black for body text and descriptive editorial excerpts.
- **Muted Text (`#74636A`):** Secondary descriptive text, meta details, volume listings, and breadcrumbs.
- **Success Green (`#287A57`):** Direct WhatsApp inquiry badges, checkout confirmations, and in-stock badges.
- **White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-gloss cards, modal surfaces, and crisp product isolation tiles.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical high-fashion serif prestige with clean, geometric modern sans-serif legibility.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for prestige banners, product collection names, brand statements, and hero quotes. Headlines feature tight leading and subtle negative tracking for larger sizes to accentuate high stroke contrast.
- **Product Titles & Interactive Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine clarity across skincare benefits, ingredients, specifications, and commerce checkout fields.
- **Prestige Eyebrows (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.18em`) using Champagne Gold (`#D8B18F`) or Deep Wine (`#270D1A`) to frame luxury groupings (e.g., `"HAUTE PARFUMERIE"`, `"BEAUTY, REFINED."`).
- **Numbers & Prices:** Use `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold tabular figures to preserve financial legibility alongside serif names.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy reflects luxury editorial publishing: spacious pacing, wide margins, deliberate asymmetrical compositions, and an uncluttered 8-point baseline rhythm.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max page content width is capped at 1380px for a boutique salon feeling.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Generous empty layout space is maintained around featured product hero shots (minimum `space-xl` top and bottom) to convey exclusivity.
- Dense data clusters (such as clinical ingredients and skincare routine sequences) use strictly aligned `space-sm` and `space-md` inner paddings to balance airy luxury with functional utility.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate ambient rose-tinted shadows, frosted cosmetic glass surfaces, and fine metallic gold boundaries rather than heavy dark drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F4`) background with occasional Cream (`#F5EBDF`) tonal sections.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) cards with a faint ambient glow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(59, 16, 36, 0.04)`. Outlined with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(216, 177, 143, 0.28)`).
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Floating Badges):** `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(59, 16, 36, 0.08)`, border transforms into subtle solid Champagne Gold (`#D8B18F`).
- **Level 3 (Modals, Dropdowns & WhatsApp Quick-Concierge):** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(39, 13, 26, 0.16)`, featuring a frosted glass overlay: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an ivory veil (`rgba(250, 247, 244, 0.85)`).
- **Luxury Gold Inset Accents:** Micro-borders with `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(216, 177, 143, 0.45)` for high-tier gift sets and VIP boutique perks.

## Shapes

The geometry blends soft organic contours reminiscent of cosmetic flacons with precise, tailored architectural elegance.

### Shape Scale
- **Cards & Product Tiles:** Set at `14px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg` level 2), creating an inviting, soft-touch tactile feel without drifting into playful toy-like roundness.
- **Action Buttons & Text Inputs:** `10px` to `12px` roundedness for structured confidence.
- **Pills & Badges:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) for status indicators, tag chips, and WhatsApp quick-action capsules.
- **Imagery Containers:** Soft radius (`16px`) with nested inner images clipped cleanly to match container contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury CTA:** Deep Burgundy (`#3B1024`) solid background, Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F4`) bold typography, 12px border radius, 48px standard height. On hover, transitions to Deep Wine (`#270D1A`) with subtle Champagne Gold box-shadow glow.
- **Secondary Champagne Button:** Transparent background, 1px border in Champagne Gold (`#D8B18F`), Dark Text (`#261923`), Champagne Gold hover fill at 12% opacity.
- **WhatsApp Direct Order CTA:** Success Green (`#287A57`) fill, White (`#FFFFFF`) bold text with inline WhatsApp icon, pill radius (`9999px`), gentle hover elevation with green aura.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Tiles:** Clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) background, 14px border radius, hairline Champagne Gold border (`rgba(216, 177, 143, 0.2)`). Displays product image with generous padding, followed by uppercase category eyebrow, Playfair Display title, volume indicator, and Burgundy price.
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** Image scales gently (1.03x over 400ms cubic-bezier), and a bottom-aligned "Quick Inquiry" or "Add to Vanity" button fades up.

### WhatsApp Concierge Badge
- Floating bottom-right circular or pill launcher featuring `#287A57` gradient, soft ping animation, and a micro-tooltip badge: "Chat with Sara’s Beauty Concierge".

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped, 24px height. Soft Blush (`#F4E4E4`) background with Deep Burgundy (`#3B1024`) text.
- **Prestige Badges:** Champagne Gold (`#D8B18F`) hairline outline, transparent center, gold uppercase text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Warm Cream (`#F5EBDF`) or White fill, 1px border (`#FAF7F4` tone blend or `#E5D9CC`), 10px corner radius, 48px height. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px border in Primary Burgundy (`#3B1024`) with an ambient `#D8B18F` outer ring.

### Lists & Accordions (Ingredients & Rituals)
- Thin 1px dividers in Champagne Gold at 20% opacity (`rgba(216, 177, 143, 0.2)`). Clean expand/collapse icons featuring fine minimalist plus/minus glyphs.
